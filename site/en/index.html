FAQ.

Is Kadro really free?

Yes. No subscription, no trial, no watermark and no sign-up. The source code is open under the GPL-3.0 license, so anyone can check it.

Is Kadro a CapCut alternative?

Kadro is modelled on CapCut Pro: timeline, transitions, effects, creator-style captions, templates, cutting long videos into Shorts. The difference is that everything is free and runs on your computer, with no account and no credits.

Does Kadro need the internet?

No. Editing, export and AI run on your computer and your videos are never uploaded. You only need the internet once, to download a speech recognition model or a voice the first time you use them.

How do I add captions automatically for free?

Open the Captions tab and choose Auto captions. Kadro recognises speech on your computer (English, Russian and 23 more languages) and places captions word by word. Pick a creator style: Hormozi, MrBeast, One word, Neon, Karaoke.

Can I export 4K video without a watermark?

Yes. Kadro exports MP4 up to 4K with no watermark, plus GIF, MP3 audio and Telegram video stickers.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 8 GB of RAM (16 GB is better for 4K and background removal), about 1 GB of disk space. A graphics card is optional; NVIDIA, AMD and Intel GPUs speed up export.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it a virus?

No. Windows shows this for programs without a paid code signing certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. Kadro's code is open on GitHub and the installer is built automatically on GitHub's servers.

Is there Kadro for Mac, Android or iPhone?

Not yet, only Windows 10 and 11.
